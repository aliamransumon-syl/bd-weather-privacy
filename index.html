BD Weather · Android

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

BD Weather is a weather app for Bangladesh. It covers every upazila (all 496, across 64 districts in 8 divisions) and shows live weather, air quality, prayer times, sun and moon times, alerts and a district map for the place you are in. The short version: we collect nothing and operate no servers. To show you the weather for where you are, the app sends your position straight from your phone to four public services, listed below. Nothing passes through us.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs. We operate no servers of our own, so there is nowhere for your data to be sent to us.

Location

Location is opt-in. The app asks for permission when you tap “Locate me” / “Use my location”. If you grant it, the app may read your location again on later launches — only while the permission stays granted — to refresh your place and weather. Revoking the permission in Android Settings stops this immediately.

The app requests precise location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION), with ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION as the fallback Android lets you choose instead. A precise fix is what lets the app name the neighbourhood or mahalla you are in and pick the nearest of the 496 upazilas (within a 100 km limit — outside Bangladesh the app tells you it cannot help). A coarse fix is only about 2 km wide. If you allow only approximate location, the app still works, with a less exact neighbourhood name.

Your position leaves the device only in the requests listed below. We never receive it.

Third-party services

The app contacts four public services:

  • Open-Meteo weather API — api.open-meteo.com. Receives your position to return your forecast. It also receives the fixed coordinates of Bangladesh's 64 district centres (not your position) for the national map and the national coolest / warmest district. Terms
  • Open-Meteo air-quality API — air-quality-api.open-meteo.com. Receives your position to return the US AQI reading.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim — nominatim.openstreetmap.org. Receives your position to return the neighbourhood / village name shown in the header. The request also carries the developer's contact email address (aliamransumon@gmail.com), which OpenStreetMap's usage policy requires from apps; it is not your email. Place names © OpenStreetMap contributors. Usage policy
  • OpenStreetMap tile servers — tile.openstreetmap.org. When you open the Map tab, your device requests map tiles. A tile request reveals the map area you are viewing and your IP address, not your exact position.

These services may log coordinates and IP addresses in their own access logs under their own policies. The app sends no device identifiers, accounts or advertising IDs. Fonts, weather icons, the map library and all other assets ship inside the app; it fetches nothing from Google Fonts or any other third-party host.

What is calculated on your device

Sunrise, sunset, moonrise, moonset, moon phase, prayer times (including Seheri and Iftar), and the heat, storm and air-quality advice shown under Alerts are all calculated on your phone from the forecast data it already holds. Nothing extra is sent anywhere to produce them.

Notifications

Daily weather notifications (tomorrow's weather at 9 PM, today's at 7 AM) are opt-in: Android asks for the notification permission when you switch them on. They run entirely on the device using Android's local-notification system — no push servers, no FCM token. Switch them off in the app or in Android Settings at any time.

Sharing

The Share button hands a short text summary of the current weather to the share sheet you choose. The app does not send it anywhere itself.

What is stored on your device

The app saves the following in its private local storage, so that it opens instantly and works offline:

  • the last downloaded weather and air-quality data for recently viewed upazilas,
  • the upazila last used and the last known neighbourhood name,
  • a map reference for that neighbourhood look-up: your position rounded to about 100 metres, kept for about 10 minutes so the app does not repeat the look-up each time it opens,
  • your chosen theme, language, temperature unit and notification setting.

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app removes it. The app's Android backup is disabled, so it does not sync to Google Drive or any other cloud backup.

Weather alerts and safety tips

Alerts and tips in the app are generated automatically from forecast data. They are general guidance, not official warnings and not medical advice. For official warnings follow the Bangladesh Meteorological Department and local authorities; in an emergency call 999.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

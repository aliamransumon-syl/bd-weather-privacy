Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

BD Weather is a weather app for eight cities in Bangladesh. This policy explains exactly what the app does with your information. The short version: nothing is collected, nothing is sent to us, and we have no servers of our own.

What the app collects

Nothing is collected by us. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs.

Location

The app asks for location permission only if you tap “Use my location” in the station picker. If you grant it, the coordinates are used on your device, for a moment, for one purpose: to work out which of the eight cities is nearest to you.

The coordinates are not stored, not logged, and not sent to us. You can use the whole app without ever granting this permission, and you can revoke it at any time in Android Settings.

Weather data

To show a forecast, the app sends a request to the Open-Meteo public weather API (api.open-meteo.com). That request contains only the coordinates of the selected city — the same request for every user. It contains no identifier about you or your device.

Open-Meteo's own privacy policy: open-meteo.com/en/terms

What is stored on your device

The app saves the following in your device's local app storage, purely so it opens instantly and works offline:

  • the last downloaded weather data for the selected city,
  • your chosen city, language and theme.

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
